The Problem

Why “Salesforce First” Isn’t Enough

01
The floor has moved

Doc gen and e-signature are no longer differentiators. Every CLM, IDP, and horizontal AI assistant now ships a version.

What used to be the product is now table stakes bundled into someone else’s platform.

02
Single-ecosystem exposure

Being tied to one ecosystem leaves us exposed if that platform absorbs or displaces the function.

Consolidation risk isn’t hypothetical — it’s the stated roadmap of every major suite vendor.

957 apps. 27% connected.

The Shift — An Open, Multi-Data-Source Platform
S-Docs orchestrates AI-driven document workflows across disparate enterprise applications.
Pillar 01
Agentic Orchestration

Coordinating multi-step document workflows across systems — autonomous within approved guardrails, with human gates on consequential actions.

Pillar 02
Document Intelligence

Documents become inputs that can be read, reasoned against, and acted on — not just artifacts that get produced and filed.

Pillar 03
Surface Independent

Agents are first-class users. Everything is invokable via the web, MCP/API, and LLM/chatbots.

This is what creates product stickiness — the more systems the platform touches, the more valuable the solution and the harder it becomes to replace.

Vignette 03 · Agent Callable

Ad-Hoc Tasks

Create a proposal. Agents can be invoked manually or triggered by external events — a Slack-based contract/proposal workflow started informally, not through a rigid pipeline.
The old way (fragmented systems): copy-pasting CRM data, sifting Slack logs for side-deals, pulling transcript details, risking compliance and version errors → slower deal velocity, high error rates, zero document trail.
With the platform (autonomous multi-system agent): auto-collects across Slack, CRM and transcripts, drafts bespoke proposals, and keeps a full audit log of exactly why, what and when — with guardrails that keep a human in final control.
