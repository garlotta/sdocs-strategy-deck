Why “Salesforce First” Isn’t Enough

01
Doc gen and e-signature are no longer differentiators

Ironclad, Icertis, and DocuSign IAM already ship both natively, and Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace now draft the documents themselves. What used to be the product is table stakes bundled into someone else’s platform.

02
Consolidation risk isn’t hypothetical

Being tied to one ecosystem leaves us exposed if that platform absorbs or displaces the function. Salesforce already ships native document generation through Omnistudio, and Agentforce 360 is built to extend that reach across the full workflow.

03
The average enterprise has 957 apps. 27% connected.

No single platform owns how work actually moves — including Salesforce. Every unconnected app means manual re-entry, broken audit trails, and work reconciled by hand across systems that were never built to talk to each other.

Five Product Vignettes

Vignette 01
Build a Workflow

Workflows are built from five configurable building blocks — Extract, Analyze, Generate, Save, Notify — mixed and matched by the user.

Vignette 02
Industry Agents

Pre-built, industry-specific agents with regulated “skills” — ready-made playbooks and extraction schemas — built in to help customers reach time-to-value faster.

Vignette 03
Ad-Hoc Tasks

Invoked on demand or triggered by an event, pulling context across disparate data sources to draft the work product in minutes.

Vignette 04
Autonomous Loops

Agents work independently, executing workflows on their own — flagging what matters and raising it to a human only when needed.

Vignette 05
Prove Every Step

Every action writes to an immutable audit trail, mapped to SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 42001 — full traceability of what happened and why.
